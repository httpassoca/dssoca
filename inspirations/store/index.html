The KC-75 is our compact daily driver: a 75% layout that keeps the function row and arrow cluster in a footprint that fits next to a trackpad. The case is machined from a single billet, bead-blasted and anodised, with a brass weight in the base for a planted feel.

Every board is hand-lubed and sound-tested before it leaves the bench. Includes a braided USB-C cable, keycap and switch puller, and spare gaskets.
